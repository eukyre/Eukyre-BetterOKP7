Eukyre’s Better OKP-7 is a mod designed around SamSWAT’s old name of the same monacre. Designed with the purpose of making the EKB OKP-7 optics (both Dovetail and Picatinny) have a selection of reticles to choose from for the user’s convenience.


<img width="1080" height="90" alt="Betterokp7 showcase" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4d4b6041-a7db-491a-9561-166b3266eb4c" />
Better OKP-7 adds a selection of 3 reticles to the optics, those being the following:

- Simple Dot - Classic, easy to use and good for target acquisition.
- Dot with a Ring - Similar to what BSG had before, but without the extra ring that doesn’t actually exist on these optics.
- The Cross - The Iconic OKP-7 reticle that everyone loves, and is preferred overall by Tarkov Players.
